2. Description of Service

MyWallet is a personal finance management application, free to use, with an optional paid subscription (MyWallet Plus). It provides:

  • Multi-account tracking (bank accounts, credit cards, cash, digital wallets, loans)

  • Spending analytics and category-based budgeting
  • Savings goal tracking (sinking funds)

The optional Google Drive backup feature:

  • Uses the Google account you sign in to MyWallet with, and needs your permission for Google Drive's App Data folder (a Drive connected before version 4.3.0 keeps backing up until you sign in)

  • Is subject to Google's Terms of Service and storage limitations
  • Stops when you sign out, and can be turned off at any time in the App or by removing MyWallet's access in your Google Account settings

  • Members see what other members add. Do not add information about other people that you do not have the right to share.
  • MyWallet only records money; it never holds, sends or receives money for you or the people you share with.
  • You can delete your MyWallet account at any time in the App or at mywallet.mdrahim.com/delete-account.
