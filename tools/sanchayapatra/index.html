How much each certificate pays

Here’s what ৳1 lakh earns at the current rates, in the band for people holding up to ৳7.5 lakh in total. “After tax” means after the 10% taken at source.

  • Paribar Sanchayapatra: ৳994.17 a month, ৳894.75 after tax, for five years.
  • Pensioner Sanchayapatra: ৳998.33 a month for five years. No tax is taken while your Pensioner certificates add up to ৳5 lakh or less.
  • 3-month profit-bearing: ৳2,955 every three months, ৳2,659.50 after tax, for three years.
  • 5-year Bangladesh Sanchayapatra: nothing until the end, then ৳59,150 in one go, ৳53,235 after tax. So ৳1,53,235 comes back.

Put ৳5 lakh in Paribar and ৳4,473.75 reaches your account every month. Over five years that’s ৳2,68,425 after tax, and the ৳5 lakh comes back at the end.

The ৳7.5 lakh line

Every scheme has two sets of rates. Up to ৳7.5 lakh you get the higher one. Above it the rate drops, to 11.80% at maturity for Paribar, Pensioner and the 5-year certificate, and 11.77% for the 3-month one.

The office counts everything you already hold across all Sanchayapatra, not only the new certificate. Say you already have ৳5 lakh and buy another ৳5 lakh of Paribar. The new one earns 11.80%, which is ৳4,916.67 a month before tax instead of ৳4,970.83. Type what you hold in Already held and the page picks the right band.

Cashing in early

You can cash in any certificate before maturity, but the profit drops to a lower rate set for the number of full years you held it. On the 5-year certificate that’s simple: ৳1 lakh cashed after three years earns 10.72% a year, which is ৳28,944 after tax, so you walk away with ৳1,28,944.

Monthly and quarterly ones hurt more, because you’ve already been paid at the full rate. The office works out what the lower rate would have paid and takes the difference out of your money. Cash in ৳5 lakh of Paribar after two years and ৳16,400 is held back, so you get ৳4,83,600. Before the first full year, there’s no profit at all. The table under the result shows every year.

About the 10% tax

Since July 2026 the 10% taken from Sanchayapatra profit counts as advance tax, not as your final tax. If your income is under the tax-free limit, you can claim it back when you file your return, which needs a TIN. The savings office gives you a certificate of the tax it took, and that’s what the return asks for.

Pensioner certificates are the exception: nothing is taken while your Pensioner holding is ৳5 lakh or less. If your office takes a different amount, type it in Tax at source.

Where the rates come from

They’re from the Internal Resources Division’s notice of 30 June 2025, in force from 1 July 2025. A cut was announced for January 2026 and withdrawn four days later, and the same rates were kept for July to December 2026. Rates are reviewed every six months, but a certificate keeps the rate it was bought at until maturity. If yours is older, type its rate in Profit rate. Last checked on 22 November 2026 against nationalsavings.gov.bd.
