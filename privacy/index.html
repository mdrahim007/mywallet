In short: MyWallet stores your financial data on your device. An optional MyWallet account keeps only your name, your email and the phones you use. An account you choose to share with family is stored in the cloud so the people you invite can see it; nothing else of yours is. We never sell your information. AI and natural language processing happen on-device only. Optional Google Drive backup is controlled entirely by you.

1. Introduction

MyWallet ("we," "our," or "the app") is a personal finance management application developed by MD Rahim. This Privacy Policy explains how MyWallet handles your information when you use our Android application (the "Service").

By using MyWallet, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Financial Data (User-Entered)

MyWallet allows you to manually enter:

  • Account information (account names, types, balances, card last four digits, currency)
  • Transaction details (amounts, merchants, categories, dates, notes)
  • Savings goals and sinking fund targets
  • Budget preferences and settings

This data is stored on your device in a local database (ObjectBox). It is not sent to us or any third party, except for an account you choose to share (see 2.7).

2.2 Secure Vault & AI Processing

MyWallet features a Secure Vault and an AI Assistant, both designed with absolute data sovereignty:

  • Secure Vault: Data stored here (like PINs) is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption before insertion into the local database.
  • AI Assistant & NLP: Natural language processing (e.g., parsing text into transactions) occurs 100% locally on your device. Your free-text queries are never sent to cloud AI providers.

2.3 SMS Messages (Optional Feature)

If you turn on Read bank SMS in MyWallet's settings, MyWallet reads SMS messages on your phone to record your financial transactions automatically.

  • What is read: SMS from banks and mobile financial services (such as bKash, Nagad, Rocket and banks). SMS from personal phone numbers are skipped based on the sender, without their content being read. Of the remaining messages, only transaction alerts are used; one-time passwords, offers and other messages are ignored.
  • What is kept: From each transaction alert, MyWallet saves the amount, whether money came in or went out, the merchant or sender, the date, the matched account and the bank's transaction reference. The message text itself is not stored.
  • Where it goes: Nowhere. Messages are processed entirely on your device. MyWallet does not upload, share, sell or use SMS content for advertising or analytics. If you use Google Drive backup, your transactions (not your messages) are saved to your own Google Drive.
  • Your control: The feature is off by default. You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Bank auto-capture, which also returns the SMS permission to Android. You can also remove the permission in Android's app settings.

2.4 Google Account Information

If you choose to use the Google Drive backup feature, MyWallet will request access to:

  • Your Google account name and email (for authentication)
  • Google Drive App Data folder (a private folder only accessible by MyWallet)

This information is used solely to authenticate with Google Drive and save/restore your backup file. We do not access any other Google account data.

2.5 Device Permissions

MyWallet may request the following permissions:

  • SMS (RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS) — Optional. Used exclusively for bank auto-capture to record transactions from incoming alerts on-device.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — For notifying you about reminders and financial milestones. Optional.
  • Biometric/Fingerprint — For optional app lock functionality. Biometric data is handled entirely by Android's BiometricPrompt API and never accessed by MyWallet directly.
  • Internet — For Google Drive backup, exchange rate updates, signing in to your MyWallet account, shared accounts and subscriptions. No personal data is transmitted beyond these specific features.

2.6 MyWallet Account (Optional)

MyWallet works without an account. When you finish setting up the app, it creates an anonymous account: a random ID with no name, email or other details, used only so you can sign in later without losing anything.

If you sign in (with Google or with a code sent to your email), we keep:

  • Your name, your email address and the sign-in methods you use
  • Your language, currency and country settings
  • The phones signed in to your account (model, Android version, app version, when last used)
  • Your plan (Free or Plus) and when it renews or ends, and when you first signed in (launch offers run from it)

Sign-in codes are sent from mywalletapps@gmail.com through Gmail. Only a salted hash of each code is stored, for 10 minutes. Signing in does not upload your transactions, balances, budgets, goals or messages.

2.7 Shared Accounts (Optional)

You can choose to share one of your accounts with people you invite. When you do:

  • That account's name, type, currency, opening balance, icon and colour, and its confirmed transactions (amount, date, type, category, merchant and note), are stored in the cloud and shown to the people you invited.
  • Each person's name and role in that account is shown to the others, and each transaction shows who added it.
  • Nothing else is shared: not your other accounts, budgets, goals or messages. Transactions captured from SMS are not shared until you confirm them.
  • Invites are codes that work once, for 7 days. An invite holds the account's name, your name and the role it gives.

You can change roles, remove people or stop sharing at any time. When you stop sharing, the account and its transactions are deleted from the cloud and from the other people's phones; your own copy stays on your phone. When someone leaves or is removed, the account is removed from their phone.

2.8 Subscriptions (MyWallet Plus)

MyWallet Plus is an optional subscription bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. We receive the purchase details Google provides (which plan, its status and when it ends) to check the purchase and turn Plus on for your account. The purchase is linked to your account by a one-way code, not by your email.

3. How We Use Your Information

All data collected is used exclusively for:

  • Displaying your financial information within the app
  • Calculating analytics, balances, and budget summaries
  • Providing AI-assisted transaction parsing
  • Automatically recording bank transactions from SMS alerts (when enabled)
  • Backing up and restoring your data via Google Drive (when you choose to)
  • Converting currencies using publicly available exchange rates
  • Signing you in, keeping your plan, and showing shared accounts to the people you invited (when you use these features)

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage: All financial data is stored in a local ObjectBox database on your device's internal storage, protected by Android's app sandbox.
  • Google Drive Backup: When enabled, backup files are stored in Google Drive's App Data folder, which is private and can only be accessed by MyWallet. Google's security and encryption standards apply.
  • Cloud (only for optional features): MyWallet accounts, shared accounts and plans are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) in Google's Mumbai region, protected by access rules so each person reads only their own account and the shared accounts they belong to. Your other data is not stored there.
  • Encryption: Data in transit (Google Drive sync, exchange rates, Firebase) uses HTTPS/TLS encryption. Google encrypts data stored in Firebase.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties.

The only external data transmission occurs when:

  • You initiate a Google Drive backup (data sent to your own Google Drive)
  • The app fetches live exchange rates from public APIs (no personal data is included in these requests)
  • You sign in with Google for backup functionality (standard OAuth flow)
  • You sign in to a MyWallet account (see 2.6)
  • You share an account: its details and transactions are visible to the people you invite (see 2.7)
  • You subscribe to MyWallet Plus through Google Play (see 2.8)

6. Data Retention

  • Your data persists on your device until you delete individual entries or uninstall the app.
  • Google Drive backups persist in your Drive until you manually delete them or revoke MyWallet's access.
  • Your MyWallet account's data is kept until you delete the account.
  • A shared account is kept until its owner stops sharing it. Transactions someone deletes are kept as deleted marks until then, so everyone's copy stays the same.
  • Sign-in codes are kept (as a hash) for 10 minutes.
  • Purchase records (plan, status and end date, linked to your account's random ID) may be kept as needed for accounting and Google Play's records.
  • After deletion, copies may remain in Google's backup systems for a limited period before they are erased.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app at all times.
  • Deletion: Delete individual transactions, accounts, or all data from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • MyWallet account: Delete it in the app (Settings → MyWallet account → Delete MyWallet account) or at mywallet.mdrahim.com/delete-account. This removes your profile, your list of phones and your sign-in; the app then asks whether to also erase the data on your phone. If you share an account with other people, stop sharing it first.
  • Shared accounts: Leave an account someone shared with you, or stop sharing one of yours, at any time.
  • Google Drive: You can disconnect Google Drive, delete backups, or revoke access from your Google Account settings.
  • Export: You can back up your data to Google Drive for portability.

8. Children's Privacy

MyWallet is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has used MyWallet, please contact us, and we will provide guidance on deleting the local data.

9. Third-Party Services

MyWallet integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In & Google Drive API — For authentication and backup. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Exchange Rate APIs — For currency conversion. Only currency codes are sent; no personal data is transmitted.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) — For MyWallet accounts, shared accounts and plans. Subject to Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Play Billing — For MyWallet Plus subscriptions.
  • Gmail — Sends the sign-in codes to your email address.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
