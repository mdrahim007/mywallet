How it works

Track expenses in three simple steps

No bank syncing. No spreadsheets. Just clear, calm control over your money.

  1. STEP 01

    Add your accounts

    Set up checking, savings, credit cards, cash and digital wallets with their opening balances — stored privately on your phone.

  2. STEP 02

    Log in seconds

    Type “Spent $15 on lunch” and Ask MyWallet categorizes and records it instantly. Or let bank SMS alerts log themselves.

  3. STEP 03

    Grow your net worth

    Stay under your category budgets and watch your assets climb, month after month.

Privacy by design

Your financial data stays with you

MyWallet is built offline-first on a local ObjectBox database, with AES-256 encryption for your most sensitive data. No servers to breach, no tracking, no compromises.

Read the privacy policy

On your phone by default

Transactions, budgets and goals live on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to back up or share.

On-device AI

Ask MyWallet runs on your phone. Your questions are never sent to a cloud AI provider.

AES-256 Secure Vault

Keep PINs and other sensitive notes encrypted at rest with AES-256, separate from everything else.

Zero ads & trackers

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no third-party data sharing. Your finances are nobody’s business.

Data independence

Your data, your rules

  • Universal CSV export. Your entire transaction history in Excel or Google Sheets, in one tap.
  • PDF reports. Clean summaries for tax season or your accountant.
  • JSON backups. Full encrypted snapshots to move devices or keep cold-storage copies.
