Features

Everything You Need to Master Your Money

From auto-logging SMS transactions to visualizing spending patterns — MyWallet has you covered.

🏦

Multi-Account Management

Bank accounts, credit cards, cash wallets, digital wallets, and loans — all beautifully organized with real-time balance tracking.

📱

Auto-Log SMS Transactions

Automatically capture bank transaction alerts from SMS. Review, confirm, or edit parsed transactions — no manual entry needed.

📊

Smart Analytics

Interactive charts showing spending by category, monthly trends, and income vs. expense breakdowns to understand your habits.

🎯

Sinking Funds & Savings Goals

Set savings targets for vacations, emergencies, or big purchases. Track progress with beautiful visual indicators.

🏷️

Tax-Ready Flagging

Flag transactions as tax-deductible with a swipe. Export clean tax reports when filing season arrives.

💱

Multi-Currency Support

Track accounts in different currencies with live exchange rate conversion. See your net worth unified in one currency.

Analytics

Understand Where Your Money Goes

Powerful visual analytics help you identify spending patterns and make smarter financial decisions.

  • ✓Spending breakdown by category with interactive donut charts
  • ✓Monthly income vs. expense trend analysis
  • ✓Credit card utilization tracking with billing cycles
  • ✓Loan amortization schedules with EMI details

Security

Your Data Stays With You

MyWallet is built with a privacy-first architecture. No servers, no tracking, no compromises.

🔒

100% Local Storage

All financial data is stored on your device only. Nothing ever leaves your phone without your explicit action.

☁️

Encrypted Backups

Optional Google Drive backup keeps your data safe. You control when and where your data is stored.

🚫

Zero Ads & Tracking

No advertisements. No analytics trackers. No third-party data sharing. Your finances are nobody's business.
