Privacy

Your money is nobody’s business.

MyWallet is built offline-first on a local database, with AES-256 encryption for your most sensitive data. No ad network, no tracker and no bank login — by design.

i.

On your phone by default

Transactions, budgets and goals live on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you choose to back up or share.

ii.

AI that stays on-device

Ask MyWallet parses and answers on your phone. Your questions never reach a cloud AI provider.

iii.

An encrypted Secure Vault

PINs, ID numbers and other sensitive details are encrypted at rest with AES-256.

iv.

No ads, ever

No advertising, no analytics trackers and no third-party data sharing.
