Delete your MyWallet account

You can also do this in the app: Settings → MyWallet account → Delete MyWallet account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, language and currency.
  • The list of phones signed in to your account.
  • Your sign-in (Google or email) with MyWallet.

Your transactions and messages stay on your phone until you uninstall the app or erase its data. Accounts others shared with you are left. If you share an account with other people, stop sharing it in the app first. Google may keep backup copies for a limited time before erasing them.
