Getting Started

From the Dashboard, tap the account card stack area. If you have no accounts, you'll see an "Add Account" card. Tap it and fill in your account details — name, type (bank, credit card, cash, etc.), currency, and opening balance. Your account card will appear on the dashboard immediately.

MyWallet supports five account types:

  • Bank Account — Checking, savings, or any bank deposit account
  • Credit Card — With credit limit, APR, billing cycle, and payment tracking
  • Cash — Track physical cash
  • Digital Wallet — PayPal, Venmo, bKash, or any e-wallet
  • Loan — With principal, interest rate, tenure, and EMI calculation

Tap the floating "+" button on the Dashboard or from an Account Detail page. Choose the transaction type (Expense, Income, or Transfer), enter the amount, select an account and category, optionally add a merchant name and note, and tap Save. You can also change the currency for cross-currency transactions.

Privacy & AI

No. MyWallet is built on an "Offline-First" philosophy. All your financial data is stored securely on your local device using a high-performance NoSQL database, and we do not transmit it to our servers. The only exception is an account you choose to share with people you invite: that account and its transactions are stored in the cloud so they can see it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The Secure Vault is a dedicated section within MyWallet for storing highly sensitive information, like PINs or SSNs. Data placed in the vault is encrypted at rest using military-grade AES-256 encryption.

The AI Assistant features a natural language processing (NLP) engine that parses your free-text input (e.g., "Spent $12 on coffee at Starbucks"). This parsing happens 100% locally on your device without sending your queries to external cloud AI providers.

Accounts & Transactions

When adding or editing a transaction, select the "Transfer" type tab. Toggle on "Internal Transfer" to select a destination account. The amount will be deducted from the source account and added to the destination. Cross-currency transfers are automatically converted at the current exchange rate.

Credit card accounts track your credit utilization, billing cycles, outstanding balance, and due dates. You'll see a breakdown of spent vs. available credit, a "Pay Now" button to record payments from your bank account, and historical billing cycles to review past statements. Set your statement date in the account settings for accurate cycle tracking.

Tap any transaction in the list to open the Edit Transaction screen. You can modify any field — amount, category, date, merchant, etc. — or delete the transaction entirely using the "Delete" button at the bottom or the trash icon in the top bar.

When adding or editing a transaction, toggle the "Tax" switch. Flagged transactions appear in the Tax Report screen (accessible from Settings), where you can review all tax-deductible expenses grouped by category and export them.

Sharing & Your Account

Open the account you want to share and tap the people icon to invite someone. Each invite is a code that works once, for 7 days. The people you invite see that account and its confirmed transactions — nothing else of yours. You can change roles, remove people or stop sharing at any time.

Open the invite link on your phone, or open MyWallet and go to Settings → MyWallet account → Join a shared account, then type the code. You need to be signed in to your MyWallet account.

Multi-Currency

Go to Settings → Currency. Select your primary currency (used for dashboard totals and net worth). You can also add secondary currencies for quick access when adding transactions.

MyWallet fetches exchange rates from public APIs. Rates are cached locally and refreshed periodically. Cross-currency transactions are converted at the rate available at the time of entry. Note: these rates are for informational purposes and may differ from your bank's actual conversion rate.

Backup & Data

Go to Settings → scroll to the Backup section. Sign in with your Google account, then tap "Backup to Google Drive." Your data is saved in Google Drive's private App Data folder, accessible only by MyWallet.

In Settings, sign in with the same Google account used for backup, then tap "Restore from Google Drive." This will replace your current local data with the backup. Make sure to back up your current data first if you want to keep it.

All locally stored data is permanently deleted when you uninstall. However, if you previously backed up to Google Drive, you can restore your data after reinstalling. We strongly recommend regular backups.

Theme & Settings

Go to Settings → Theme. You can choose Light, Dark, or System Default (follows your phone's theme setting). The theme change applies instantly across all screens.

Tap the eye icon (👁) next to "Available Balance" on the Dashboard. This hides all amounts across the balance summary. Tap again to reveal. The setting persists across app restarts.
